dice-o-matic

Jamb dice game with real 3D dice — for Android, iPhone and iPad.

Shake the dice in a cup and pour them onto the felt, keep the dice you want and write your result on the classic four-column score sheet. Play alone, with up to four players on one device, or against the phone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove the ads?
Tap REMOVE ADS in the main menu. It is a single one-time purchase that removes all ads permanently.

I bought "Remove ads" but see ads on a new device.
On iPhone and iPad, tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the main menu while signed in with the same Apple Account. On Android the purchase is restored automatically for the same Google account.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Tap Ad privacy options in the main menu (shown in the EEA and the UK). On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where are my saved games?
Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded; uninstalling the app deletes them.
