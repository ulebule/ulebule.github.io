Fruit Stack

A relaxing fruit merge puzzle — for Android, iPhone and iPad.

Drag to aim and let go to drop a fruit into the jar. Two of the same fruit merge into a bigger one, from a blueberry all the way up to a watermelon. If a fruit stays above the red line for too long, the jar is full and the game is over. In English, Slovenian, German, Italian, French and Russian.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Touch the screen and drag left or right to move the fruit above the jar. Lift your finger to drop it. The next fruit is shown at the top right, and the strip at the bottom shows which fruits you have reached.

How do I change the language, sound, music or vibration?
Tap the pause button at the top right. Tap a flag to change the language; until you choose one, the game follows your phone's language.

How do I remove the ads?
Tap Remove ads in the pause menu or on the game over screen. It is a single one-time purchase that removes all ads permanently.

I bought "Remove ads" but see ads on a new device.
On iPhone and iPad, tap Restore purchase in the pause menu while signed in with the same Apple Account. On Android the purchase is restored automatically for the same Google account.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Tap Ad privacy in the pause menu (shown in the EEA and the UK). On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
